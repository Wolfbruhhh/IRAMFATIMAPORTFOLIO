A LITTLE ABOUT ME

People first.
Always.

I believe the most meaningful work begins with understanding people.

Today, I bring that belief to my role as a Senior Customer Relationship Manager (CRM) at Aditya Birla Capital. My interests in financial services, communication and business development meet in one place: building relationships grounded in trust.

My journey has also taken me from school sports grounds and college societies to poetry stages across Delhi University. Along the way, I’ve learned to listen closely, speak with intention and show up for the people around me.

With a background in History and a love for storytelling, I bring curiosity about people and their perspectives into both my professional and creative life.
